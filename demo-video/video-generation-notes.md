# How this video was made

## Tool choice

None of the four suggested tools (Runcast, DemoTape, NeuraScreen, Demo Studio)
was installed. With a deadline the same day, the safer choice was a pipeline
built only from tools already on the build machine:

- **Playwright + headless Chrome** to drive the real app and to draw every frame
- **ffmpeg** to encode, join and mix
- a small **Python** script to generate an original music bed (no third-party music)

Nothing was downloaded and executed from an unknown source. The only packages
fetched were web fonts from npm (`@fontsource/noto-sans-devanagari`,
`@fontsource/noto-sans-tamil`, `@fontsource/kalam`).

## Recording workflow

1. Reset the demo data (`python -m scripts.seed_fake_data`) and start the backend.
2. `capture.py` logs in with the demo account and drives the app at a phone-sized
   viewport (430 × 932, 2× pixel density), saving a JPEG at each step:
   typed commands, replies, the customer khata, the voice flow, the "udhaar du?"
   verdicts, the dashboard and a collections call. 93 frames.
3. `stage.html` composes every 1920 × 1080 frame: story scenes, the phone frame
   with the real screenshots, captions, the architecture diagram and the end card.
4. `render.py` films `stage.html` frame by frame at 30 fps, one MP4 per scene,
   then joins the scenes without re-encoding.
5. `music.py` writes the music bed; ffmpeg mixes it under the picture.
6. `add_narration.py` (run on your machine with your key) voices the script with
   Gnani Timbre and mixes it in, ducking the music under the voice.

## What is real and what is not

| Part | Status |
|---|---|
| Every app screen | Real capture of this repo, demo data, real backend replies |
| Typed commands | Real: typed into the app's text box |
| Voice scene | App, reply and ledger update are real; the **microphone input is injected** (a headless browser has no microphone). Said on screen. |
| Collections call | Real app flow; the **customer is simulated** by the app's call simulator. Said on screen and in the narration. |
| Reasoning model | Shown as GPT-OSS 20B on Groq. The recording itself ran on the rule engine (no LLM key in the recording environment). |
| Statistics | Real, sourced on screen (see narration-script.md) |
| Music | Original, generated by `music.py` |
| Narration | Not in `final-bol-khata-demo.mp4`; added by `add_narration.py` with Gnani Timbre |

## Output

- `final-bol-khata-demo.mp4` — 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, H.264, about 2:00, music + burnt-in captions
- `final-bol-khata-demo-narrated.mp4` — same with the Gnani Timbre voiceover (made on your machine)
- `screenshots/` — selected real app frames

## Limitations

- The live microphone path (Gnani Prisma) is not in the footage. For the strongest
  entry, record 10–15 s of yourself speaking a command into the app on your Mac
  (QuickTime → New Screen Recording) and put it in your post alongside the video.
- Captions are burnt in; there is no separate subtitle track.
