# Bol-Khata — demo video storyboard

1920 × 1080 · 30 fps · 2:00. Story arc: the size of the problem in real numbers →
the idea → a genuine demo of the app → the impact. Every app screen is a real
capture of this repository running against its seeded demo data.

Look: Bol-Khata's own identity — deep navy story scenes, the app's blue, the
app's red for udhaar, and a voice waveform as the recurring motif (the product
is a voice). The old khata is an Indian school notebook with a red binding,
written in Kalam, an Indian handwriting typeface.
